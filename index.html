Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Pockit is an iOS app that helps App Store creators view Apple App Store Connect sales, revenue, downloads, ratings, reviews, widgets, and related business insights in one place. We design Pockit around local storage and user control.

2. Information You Provide

To connect to App Store Connect, you may enter Apple Connect API information such as Issuer ID, Key ID, Vendor Number, and a private key. You may also choose app filters, currency, widget preferences, Face ID settings, notification settings, and display preferences.

These credentials and preferences are stored on your device. Pockit does not operate a separate server to collect, store, or process your App Store Connect credentials.

3. App Store Connect Data

After you connect your account, Pockit may request data from Apple's App Store Connect APIs and related Apple services, including app metadata, sales and proceeds reports, downloads, ratings, reviews, territories, currencies, and app status information.

This data is used only to display analytics inside Pockit and its widgets. Cached data is stored locally on your device to improve speed and reduce repeated network requests.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

Pockit does not collect or sell personal data for advertising or tracking. We do not collect contacts, photos, camera content, microphone recordings, precise location, browsing history, or device identifiers for third-party tracking.

5. Network Requests and Third-Party Services

Pockit connects to Apple services when you sync App Store Connect data, validate subscription purchases, open Apple's standard terms, or use system features provided by Apple. We do not share your data with advertising networks or third-party analytics providers.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage: Credentials, caches, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • System protection: Pockit relies on iOS sandboxing, file protection, Keychain or local secure storage where applicable, and system-level encryption.
  • User control: You can delete API information and locally cached data from the app settings.
  • No resale: We do not sell, rent, or trade your App Store Connect data.

7. Widgets and Notifications

Pockit may write selected summary data to the app group container so iOS widgets can display your chosen metrics. If you enable notifications, Pockit may use local notifications to inform you about app data updates. Notification permission can be managed in iOS Settings.

8. Subscriptions and Purchases

Pockit may use Apple's StoreKit framework to process purchases or subscriptions. Purchase processing is handled by Apple. We receive only the information needed to unlock app features and restore purchases.

9. Children's Privacy

Pockit is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored credentials, settings, and cached analytics remain on your device until you delete them, reset the app, or uninstall the app. Because Pockit does not maintain a separate backend account system, there is no server-side account data for us to delete.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: jason2k@126.com

隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年5月31日

1. 引言

Pockit 是一款帮助 App Store 开发者查看 Apple App Store Connect 销量、收入、下载量、评分、评价、小组件及相关经营数据的 iOS 应用。我们围绕本地存储和用户控制来设计 Pockit。

2. 您提供的信息

为了连接 App Store Connect，您可能会填写 Apple Connect API 信息，例如 Issuer ID、Key ID、Vendor Number 和私钥内容。您也可以设置 App 筛选、货币、小组件偏好、Face ID、通知和显示偏好。

这些凭证和偏好会存储在您的设备本机。Pockit 不运营额外服务器来收集、保存或处理您的 App Store Connect 凭证。

3. App Store Connect 数据

连接账号后，Pockit 可能会向 Apple 的 App Store Connect API 及相关 Apple 服务请求数据，包括 App 元数据、销量和收入报告、下载量、评分、评价、地区、货币和 App 状态信息。

这些数据仅用于在 Pockit 应用内和小组件中展示数据分析。缓存数据会保存在您的设备本机，以提高打开速度并减少重复网络请求。

4. 我们不会收集的信息

Pockit 不会为了广告或追踪目的收集或出售个人数据。我们不会收集联系人、照片、相机内容、麦克风录音、精确位置、浏览历史或用于第三方追踪的设备标识符。

5. 网络请求和第三方服务

当您同步 App Store Connect 数据、验证订阅购买、打开 Apple 标准条款或使用 Apple 系统功能时，Pockit 会连接 Apple 服务。我们不会将您的数据分享给广告网络或第三方分析服务商。

6. 数据存储与安全

  • 本地存储： 凭证、缓存和设置保存在您的设备本机。
  • 系统保护： Pockit 依赖 iOS 沙盒、文件保护、适用情况下的钥匙串或本地安全存储，以及系统级加密能力。
  • 用户控制： 您可以在应用设置中删除 API 信息和本机缓存数据。
  • 不转售： 我们不会出售、出租或交易您的 App Store Connect 数据。

7. 小组件与通知

Pockit 可能会将您选择的摘要数据写入 App Group 容器，以便 iOS 小组件展示对应指标。如果您开启通知，Pockit 可能会使用本地通知提醒您 App 数据更新。通知权限可以在 iOS 设置中管理。

8. 订阅与购买

Pockit 可能会使用 Apple 的 StoreKit 框架处理购买或订阅。购买流程由 Apple 处理。我们仅接收用于解锁功能和恢复购买所需的信息。

9. 儿童隐私

Pockit 并非面向 13 岁以下儿童的应用，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

10. 数据保留与删除

本地存储的凭证、设置和缓存分析数据会保留在您的设备上，直到您主动删除、重置应用或卸载应用。由于 Pockit 不维护独立的后端账号系统，因此不存在需要我们删除的服务器端账号数据。

11. 隐私政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更都会发布在本页面，并更新日期。
